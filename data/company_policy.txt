Invoice approval policy

Invoices above 100000 PKR require manager approval before payment. Invoices at or below 100000 PKR can follow the standard finance verification workflow.

Duplicate invoice numbers must be investigated before payment. A repeated invoice number should never be automatically approved.

Expense evidence policy

Every reimbursable expense should have supporting evidence such as an invoice or receipt. Missing evidence requires finance review before reimbursement.

Transaction classification

Finance staff should classify transactions using the company's chart of accounts. If the correct category is uncertain, the transaction should be flagged for finance review rather than guessed.

Cash flow controls

Cash outflows should be reconciled against the transaction register. Material differences between source documents and the transaction register require investigation before management reporting.
